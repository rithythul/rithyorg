On Saturday night, walking the busy streets of Siem Reap, Nady said something beautiful.

I'll quote it here to remember: *"You have made the choice. Your goal in life is to understand why you made those choices."*

It sounds like the Oracle in *The Matrix*.

Sometimes we make choices on impulse, without fully understanding them, but we make them anyway.

To live a more philosophical life, we should study why we made those choices. Remember this when we ask questions like "What is the meaning of life?" or "Who am I?"
