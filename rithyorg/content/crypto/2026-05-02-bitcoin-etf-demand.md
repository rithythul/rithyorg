<p>April's spot Bitcoin ETF net inflows hit $2.44B — a record month. Meanwhile, MicroStrategy quietly stacked another 3,273 BTC, pushing their total stash past 200,000.</p>
<p>Two things stand out. First, the ETF demand isn't slowing down. We're past the "launch hype" phase and inflows are accelerating. Second, MicroStrategy's strategy of debt-funded accumulation is still working — their avg cost basis is miles below current price.</p>
<p>The real signal: institutional allocators who sat out 2024's ETF launch are now entering. April's numbers suggest the next wave of traditional finance money is arriving.</p>
