# Mystery Whale Dumps $1.3B BlackRock ETF as CME Goes 24/7 - May 28, 2026

*Daily curated crypto news digest for May 28, 2026.*

## 1. Mystery Whale Dumps $1.29 Billion of BlackRock Bitcoin ETF in Dark Pool Block Trade
**Severity:** IMPORTANT
**Source:** [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/05/27/whale-alert-someone-dumped-usd1-29-billion-of-blackrock-s-bitcoin-etf-in-a-dark-pool-trade) / [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/bitcoin-below-76k-blackrock-ibit-dark-pool-trade/) / [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/blackrock-ibit-dark-pool-trade-bitcoin/) / [MoneyCheck](https://moneycheck.com/bitcoin-btc-price-plummets-after-massive-1-3b-blackrock-etf-dark-pool-trade/)
**Published:** May 27, 2026

An unknown investor dumped approximately $1.29 billion worth of BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) shares — roughly 29.2 million shares at ~$43 each — through a single dark pool block trade at 14:30 UTC on May 27. The transaction was confirmed by Bloomberg ETF analyst Eric Balchunas and estimated by Galaxy Research's Alex Thorn to equal roughly 16,400 BTC. Bitcoin dropped 1.5% within 10 minutes of the trade crossing, briefly slipping below $76,000. The single block exceeded IBIT's typical daily trading volume. It remains unclear whether the trade was a single large holder liquidating or a portfolio rebalance. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have now recorded $2.26 billion in outflows over the past two weeks, with the week ending May 22 marking the third-largest weekly outflow of 2026 at $1.26 billion. BlackRock's IBIT alone shed approximately $1.01 billion over that period, with $333 million in outflows on Tuesday alone.

## 2. CME Group Launches 24/7 Crypto Futures and Options Trading Starting May 29
**Severity:** POSITIVE
**Source:** [CME Group](http://investor.cmegroup.com/news-releases/news-release-details/cme-group-launch-247-cryptocurrency-futures-and-options-trading) / [Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/cme-group-to-launch-24-7-crypto-futures-options-trading/) / [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/8cf8b-cme-24-7-crypto-trading-watershed) / [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/cme-group-to-launch-24-7-trading-for-crypto-futures-and-options-on-may-29/)
**Published:** February 19 / May 2, 2026 (Launch: May 29, 2026)

CME Group, the world's largest regulated derivatives marketplace, will begin 24-hour, seven-day-a-week trading for its cryptocurrency futures and options contracts starting Friday, May 29, 2026 at 4:02 p.m. Central Time. The move, originally announced in February and pending regulatory review, brings regulated crypto derivatives in line with spot markets that already trade around the clock. CME reported $3 trillion in notional crypto derivatives volume in 2025 and a 46% year-over-year increase in average daily crypto futures volume in 2026. The shift is widely seen as a watershed moment for institutional crypto infrastructure, eliminating the weekend gap that has historically created volatility dislocations between CME and spot markets.

## 3. Bridge Exploits Push 2026 Cross-Chain Losses Past $329 Million
**Severity:** IMPORTANT
**Source:** [Protos](https://protos.com/bridge-hacks-back-in-vogue-as-verus-exploit-brings-2026-total-to-329m/) / [Tron Weekly](https://www.tronweekly.com/ethereum-exploit-verus-ethereum-bridge-suffers/) / [CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/05/18/crypto-bridge-hacks-top-328m-in-2026-as-cross-chain-exploits-accelerate/) / [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/verus-suffers-11-5m-hack-as-bridge-related-exploits-hit-329m-in-2026/)
**Published:** May 18, 2026

Eight major bridge-related exploits through mid-May 2026 have resulted in cumulative losses of $328.6 million, according to PeckShieldAlert. The most recent incident was the Verus-Ethereum bridge hack on May 18, which drained approximately $11.58 million after an attacker submitted a forged cross-chain transfer message that bypassed verification. Security platform Blockaid first flagged the active exploit. These bridge attacks follow the much larger KelpDAO/LayerZero rsETH exploit in April, which drained $292 million in what remains the largest DeFi hack of 2026, attributed to North Korea's Lazarus Group. The recurring pattern of cross-chain bridge vulnerabilities continues to be one of the most costly attack vectors in crypto, with 79 exploit incidents recorded since the beginning of 2026.

## 4. HYPE Hits All-Time High as Hyperliquid ETFs Drive Institutional Inflows
**Severity:** POSITIVE

**Published:** May 14–26, 2026

Hyperliquid's native HYPE token reached an all-time high above $62 on May 26, driven by a combination of the first U.S. spot Hyperliquid ETF launches and the protocol's mechanical buyback mechanism. Bitwise launched the BHYP ETF on NYSE on May 15, with 21Shares (THYP) following on May 14 — together producing what BanklessTimes called "the strongest altcoin ETF debut of 2026." Bitwise's BHYP reached $30.5 million in AUM within five trading days. The firm also announced it will dedicate 10% of BHYP management fees to purchasing and staking HYPE tokens on its corporate balance sheet. Hyperliquid's Assistance Fund recycles 97-99% of trading fees into continuous HYPE buybacks, creating sustained buy-side pressure independent of ETF flows. FalconX reported that Hyperliquid is expanding beyond perpetual futures into pre-IPO trading, prediction contracts, and tokenized real-world assets, positioning it as a challenger to traditional exchanges.

## 5. Bitcoin ETF Gauge Enters "High-Risk" Zone Amid Geopolitical Selling Pressure
**Severity:** IMPORTANT
**Source:** [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/05/27/live-markets-bitcoin-remains-under-pressure-as-korea-s-sk-hynix-joins-micron-in-usd1-trillion-club) / [Bitcoin Foundation](https://bitcoinfoundation.org/news/crypto-etfs-news/etp-may-outflows/) / [Investing News](https://investingnews.com/cryptocurrency-market-recap/) / [The Central Bulletin](https://thecentralbulletin.com/tcb-state-of-crypto-may-2026/)
**Published:** May 27, 2026

Bitcoin's exchange-traded fund demand gauge has moved into a "high-risk" zone, CoinDesk reported, as U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have accumulated a net of only 4,500 BTC since the start of 2026. May reversed the buying pattern built through March and April, with geopolitical tensions from the ongoing U.S.-Iran conflict and surging equity markets drawing capital away from digital assets. Bitcoin fell to $74,300 at its weekly low before recovering slightly to $75,804. The "sell the news" dynamic following the CLARITY Act's Senate Banking Committee advancement on May 14 contributed to the risk-off posture. Memory chip stocks like SK Hynix and Micron attracted significant investor attention, with crypto markets settling into what analysts described as "apathy." U.S. crypto ETPs lost $1.47 billion between May 16 and May 22, the second consecutive negative week and the third-largest outflow since January.
