# The Survivors

Non-fiction about Cambodia's startups from the early days, told through smallworld, the people, and the things built there.
Before the 2000s Cambodia had mostly traditional companies and NGOs; startups began in the 2000s and tech startups around 2010, and most of those still running started then.
smallworld built startups first-hand and watched others from the sidelines: the ups and downs, and what works and what does not.
Founders who started and failed appear unnamed.
Only the survivors write the story, and startups survive about as rarely as life itself does.
