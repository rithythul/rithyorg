# Chapter Revision Workflow

Before revising any chapter, you must:

1. Review profile.md to understand tone, voice, and style preferences.
2. Review Manual_of_Style.md for all current style rules.
3. Review Manuscript_Outline.md to ensure consistency with overall structure and chapter objectives.

Then follow this 8‑step multi‑pass revision process for each chapter:

1. **Segment the draft** into logical paragraphs using natural breaks (topic shifts, temporal markers, or speaker changes).
2. **Replace em‑dashes** with commas or rephrased clauses (Rule 1).
3. **Expand acronyms** at first occurrence with plain‑language equivalents (Rule 2).
4. **Delete or rephrase bracketed notations**—integrate information into the text or move to footnotes.
5. **Rewrite sentences** that end with causal conjunctions (because, since, as, due to), placing the cause clause at the start (Rule 7).
6. **Run a clarity pass**: verify every technical term is explained through concrete detail or analogy (Rules 3, 4, 11, 12).
7. **Measure and adjust sentence length**, targeting a mix of 8‑12 words with strong verbs and minimal adverbs (Rules 8, 9).
8. **Validate paragraph endings**—each should finish on a tangible image, action, or sensory detail (Rule 6).

This repeatable skill ensures consistent, high‑quality output across all chapters.
